For a few years my focus was on getting features working. Lately the questions that interest me are different: why is this system shaped this way, what will hurt in two years, and what would I change?

## What I'm doing about it

- Reading widely and writing short notes on what I learn.
- Redesigning systems I already know, and asking what the trade-offs were.
- Publishing the notes here so I'm forced to make them clear.

## Why write publicly

Explaining an idea in plain words is the fastest way to find out whether I understand it. If a note is confusing to me a month later, it was probably confusing when I wrote it.

Expect a mix of longer articles and shorter, evolving notes in the [bliki](/bliki/).
